{% call modal('scoringModal', 'How scoring works', '560px') %}

Each habit keeps its own running total, replayed from the day you added it.

  • +{{ score.rules.hit }} every day you follow it
  • {{ score.rules.miss }} every day you miss — never below zero

Net days is days followed minus days missed. Every {{ score.rules.tier_length }} net days moves the habit up a tier, and each tier is worth one more point a day — and one more on a miss. A single miss costs one net day, not the whole climb.

Rest days are earned by showing up: set how many you get per stretch of days followed. Spending one covers a day as if you had done it — the streak holds and you keep the point.

Miss {{ score.rules.reset_after }} days in a row and the habit resets to zero. It stays on your list — follow it again and it starts building from scratch. Leave it resting long enough and it slips back into your ideas.
